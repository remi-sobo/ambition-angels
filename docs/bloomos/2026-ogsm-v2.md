# Ambition Angels 2026 OGSM (v2)

*The strategic plan, rebased to necessity. Source of truth for the BloomOS strategy section. Last built June 26, 2026.*

Four objectives (kept as-is). Refreshed goals, strategies, and measures underneath. Three KPIs carry the year, marked WALL.

## The frame

We are a platform and content provider built around the trusted adult. Partners run the program twice a week; they own the relationship and the incentives. We provide the platform, the content, and the Adult Guide dashboard, and we keep our recurring cost per teen low on purpose. The teens who change are the ones with an adult tracking their progress, so we equip the adult rather than chase the teen.

The money rebases to that reality. The committed floor that funds the necessary plan is about **$1.12M** ($1,117,782), driven by the non-negotiable $400K platform build and a net-new program lead. Another ~$250K of staged tiers (video, marketing, optional contract engagement) unlocks as money lands, for an everything-on envelope near $1.37M. Today: $196K secured, ~$405K weighted pipeline, $113K in the bank, roughly 2.3 months of runway. The honest number is **~$516K of net-new money to clear the floor** on a realistic close, because the build cannot be cut.

## Objective 1 — Deliver an impactful program experience
*Owner: Remi.* 2026: lead with careers exposed as the hard outcome (four internships, four careers a year), with Future Orientation Score as the directional belief indicator, not a validated instrument this year. Finish the pilot and bring the dashboard to parents.

**Goal 1.1 — Ship the school-ready platform and bring the dashboard to parents.**
Strategies: finish the two-school pilot to completion; ship the web-based school platform for twice-a-week in-program use (the parent dashboard is already built, the school platform is in build); extend the Adult Guide dashboard from program leaders to parents.
Measures: web platform shipped (target yes, by Q3); pilot completed (target 1); parents with an active dashboard (target 100).

**Goal 1.2 — Prove depth: four internships, four careers a year.**
Strategies: lock the rhythm of four internships per year, one per quarter, twice a week in program plus outside; grow the internship content library to support it.
Measures: careers exposed per active teen (target 4); FOS lift, directional (target hold at 14%, **confirm 14 vs the 10 Koshland committed**).

**Goal 1.3 — Launch Ambition Coach.**
Strategies: seat the program lead to own it; recruit and train mentors sourced from corporate employee engagement and the donor base (same asset, two buyers); run the first full cohorts.
Measures: teens through Ambition Coach (target 50).

## Objective 3 — Demonstrate the most effective and efficient recruitment strategy
*Owner: Remi.* 2026: partner-led and adult-driven. Activate the ~20 current partners to real twice-a-week usage, convert the 50-partner pipeline, and design a platform a program can run without us in the room.

**Goal 3.1 — Reach 1,000 teens active twice a week.** WALL (the headline) and the partner lever.
Strategies: activate the current partners to a real twice-a-week cadence; convert the 50-partner pipeline at about three conversations per partner; ship the partner integration toolkit; run place-based recruitment where a funder cares about a geography (Oakland for Koshland, and beyond).
Measures: **teens active twice a week (target 1,000) WALL**; partners running twice a week (target ~20, the lever).

**Goal 3.2 — Make the trusted-adult layer real and durable.**
Strategies: stand up MOUs and data-sharing agreements where none exist today; define the adult engagement model with parents and mentors as primary drivers.
Measures: deeply engaged teens with a connected adult (depth guardrail, target 400, **confirm 250 to 500**); partners with a signed MOU or data agreement (target 8, current 0).

## Objective 4 — Build a strong infrastructure for a sustainable organization
*Owner: Shannon.* A board that participates in fundraising and oversight; a team, systems, and culture that operate without founder dependency; consistent compliance as the org scales.

**Goal 4.1 — Make the three hires in sequence.**
Strategies: hire the program lead first (it gates Coach and partnerships); rehire the curriculum director second; add the fundraising hire later, with Remi holding fundraising until then.
Measures: key hires made, program lead and curriculum (target 2, current 0).

**Goal 4.2 — Reduce founder dependency.**
Strategies: build the board into fundraising and oversight; run the org on BloomOS; keep compliance filings and rhythms on schedule.
Measures: compliance items on time (target 100%).
