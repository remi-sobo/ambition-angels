# Ambition Angels 2026–27 OGSM (v3)

*Plan year: June 2026 through June 2027. Supersedes the 2026 calendar-year OGSM (v2).
Source of truth: BloomOS strategy section.*

Six objectives. Three KPIs carry the year, marked **WALL**.

---

## The frame

We are a platform and content provider built around the trusted adult. Partners run the program twice a week. They own the relationship and the room. We provide the platform, the content, and the Adult Guide dashboard, and we keep recurring cost per teen low on purpose.

Three things changed this year and the plan reflects them.

**The platform moved to the web.** Teens use it in a classroom, on school computers, with an adult who is paid to be in the room. That settles where this lives and who buys it.

**The partnership model is settled.** Schools at $25,000, programs at $10,000, sites of 300 to 500 students, and only where an advisory or homeroom block already exists. That makes MOUs and data agreements a dependency rather than paperwork.

**Ambition Coach became a product.** A teen who finishes a career track applies for four sessions with a working professional and leaves with a written plan. The plan goes to her adult guide.
